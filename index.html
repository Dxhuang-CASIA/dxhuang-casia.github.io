I'm a final-year Ph.D. student in the Medical Robotics Lab at the State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), supervised by Prof. Zeng-Guang Hou (IEEE Fellow) and Prof. Xiao-Hu Zhou.
In October 2026, I was a visiting student at Hong Kong Baptist University (HKBU) under the supervision of Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung (IEEE Fellow).
I received my B.S. degree in electronic engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT) in 2022.

I expect to graduate in 2027 and am actively seeking postdoctoral and industry research opportunities in AI4Healthcare.

🧐 Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, medical imaging, and clinical applications. I am interested in:

  • Medical Foundation Models: scalable representation learning and multimodal learning for healthcare
  • Medical Image Analysis: segmentation, registration, and synthesis
  • AI-assisted Intervention: intelligent perception and navigation for vascular interventional procedures
