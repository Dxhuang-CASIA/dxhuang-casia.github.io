🧐 Research Interests

My research centers on AI-driven medical image analysis for safer and more effective vascular interventions. My current interests include:

  • Medical image processing: multimodal registration, vessel segmentation, and stenosis detection
  • Computer vision: unsupervised representation learning and generative models
